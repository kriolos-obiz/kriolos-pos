1. Overview

In the original Openbravo POS / uniCenta architecture inherited by KriolOS POS, data access and transactional business logic are coupled directly to the database via 13 monolithic DataLogic* classes located in kriolos-opos-domain.

These classes are registered as singletons inside BeanContainer (extending BeanFactoryDataSingle or BeanFactoryApp). Over years of evolution, individual DataLogic classes accumulated multiple unrelated business concerns, resulting in broad scope anti-patterns, cross-domain entity coupling, and hidden dependencies.

This document serves as the baseline record of the legacy data layer architecture to guide the long-term objective of extracting clean, well-defined Business Modules.

3. Scope Analysis & Anti-Patterns

3.1. DataLogicSales: The Super-Monolith (1,608 LOC)

DataLogicSales is the central God Object of the legacy system. Despite its name, it directly queries or mutates 29 database tables spanning 9 distinct business domains:

  1. Ticket Lifecycle & Checkout (Core Sales Domain):

    • Methods: loadTicket, loadLastTicket, saveTicket, deleteTicket, getTicketsList, getReprintTicket, getReprintTicketList.

    • Sequence Numbering: getNextTicketIndex, getNextTicketRefundIndex, getNextPickupIndex, resetPickup.

  2. Inventory & Stock Diary (Inventory Domain):

    • Methods: getProductStockState, getProductStockList, saveStockDiary, getStockDiaryInsert, getStockDiaryDelete, findProductStock.

    • Issue: Stock diary recording and stock level checking are core inventory operations, yet they are stranded inside DataLogicSales rather than DataLogicInventory.

  3. Taxes & Fiscality (Finance/Tax Domain):

    • Methods: getTableTaxes, getTableTaxCategories, getTableTaxCustCategories, getTaxList, getTaxListAll, getTaxCategoriesList.

  4. Customers & Debt (CRM Domain):

    • Methods: getCustomerInsert, getCustomerUpdate, getCustomerDelete, getCustomersTransactionList, updateCustomerDebt.

    • Issue: Duplicates customer CRUD already provided by DataLogicCustomers.

  5. Locations & Warehouses (Master Data Domain):

    • Methods: getTableLocations, getLocationsList, getLocationsListAll.

  6. Restaurant Layout (Hospitality Domain):

    • Methods: getFloorsList, getFloorTablesList (queries floors and places tables).

  7. Product Master Data & UOM (PIM / Catalog Domain):

    • Methods: getTableUom, getUomInfoById, getUomList, getAttributeSetList, addProductsToCatalogWithCategoryId, removeProductsFromCatalogWithCategoryId, getProductImage.

  8. Cash Movements & Drawers (Treasury Domain):

    • Methods: getPaymentMovementInsert, getPaymentMovementDelete.

  9. Security & Audit Logging (Surveillance Domain):

    • Methods: addTicketLineRemoved, addTicketDeleted.

DataLogicSales contains tight couplings that bypass the bean container: * Instantiates DataLogicCustomers directly inside transaction logic:

+

DataLogicCustomers customerDataLogic = new DataLogicCustomers();
customerDataLogic.init(sessionDB);

  • Calls static methods across other DataLogics:

    • DataLogicVouchers.updateVoucherNonActive(…​)

    • DataLogicPIM.getProductsBundle(…​)

    • DataLogicPIM.getProductInfoExtById(…​)

3.2. DataLogicSystem: The Catch-All Trap (537 LOC)

Located in com.openbravo.pos.forms, DataLogicSystem combines platform-level infrastructure with high-level application logic:

  • System Metadata: getDBVersion(), findVersion().

  • Security & Auth (Overlapping with DataLogicAdmin):

  • listPeopleVisible(), findPeopleByCard(), getUser(), execChangePassword().

  • getPermissions(), findRolePermissions(), execUpdatePermissions().

  • UI Resources & Assets: getTableResources(), getResourceAsBinary(), getResourceAsText(), setResource().

  • Kitchen Orders (Duplicate of DataLogicOrders):

  • Implements addOrder(), updateOrder(), and deleteOrder(). DataLogicOrders only contains addOrder() and is largely orphaned.

  • Vouchers (Duplicate of DataLogicVouchers):

  • Implements getVouchersActiveList() directly against the vouchers table rather than using DataLogicVouchers.

3.3. DataLogicInventory vs DataLogicSales: Anemic Fragmentation

  • DataLogicInventory is only 72 lines long and contains only 3 static statements for stocklevel and stockcurrent.

  • All meaningful inventory operations—ledger transactions in stockdiary, stock valuations, multi-location stock calculations, and inventory adjustment statements—live inside DataLogicSales.

  • Consequently, inventory UI screens (such as StockManagement and StockDiaryEditor) are forced to inject DataLogicSales, DataLogicSystem, DataLogicSuppliers, and DataLogicPIM concurrently.

3.4. DataLogicReceipts: Misnamed Component (420 LOC)

  • The class name suggests receipt generation, printing, or fiscal receipt storage.

  • In reality, it deals 100% with networked shared/parked tickets (sharedtickets table):

  • getSharedTicket(), getSharedTicketList(), insertSharedTicket(), updateSharedTicket(), lockSharedTicket(), unlockSharedTicket().

  • Standard customer receipts and receipt reprints are handled entirely by DataLogicSales (loadTicket, getReprintTicket).

3.5. DataLogicCustomers: Hospitality Bleed

DataLogicCustomers manages customer accounts, cards, and tax numbers, but also includes table reservation management: * getReservationsList(), getReservationsInsert(), getReservationsUpdate(), getReservationsDelete(). * Interacts with reservations and reservation_customers tables (CHAIRS, DATENEW, etc.). * Table reservations belong to the Hospitality / Restaurant domain, not the generic CRM domain.

6. Decoupling Roadmap

6.1. Phase 1: Deduplication & Bean Isolation

  1. Consolidate Kitchen Orders: Remove duplicate order methods in DataLogicSystem and consolidate onto DataLogicOrders (or target HospitalityDomainService).

  2. Consolidate Vouchers: Remove direct voucher queries in DataLogicSystem and DataLogicSales, routing them through DataLogicVouchers.

  3. Eliminate Ad-Hoc Instantiations: Replace new DataLogicCustomers() inside DataLogicSales with type-safe app.getBean(DataLogicCustomers.class).

6.2. Phase 2: Domain Extraction & Backwards-Compatible Facades

  1. Extract Inventory Logic (Completed): Moved saveStockDiary, getProductStockState, getProductStockList, and findProductStock from DataLogicSales into DataLogicInventory. Migrated LocationsPanel, StockDiaryPanel, StockDiaryEditor, StockManagement, ProductsEditor, and StockQtyImport to DataLogicInventory.

  2. Extract Tax Logic (Completed): Extracted tax queries (getTaxList, getTaxCategoriesList, getTaxCustCategoriesList) and table definitions (getTableTaxes, getTableTaxCategories, getTableTaxCustCategories) into dedicated DataLogicTax (com.openbravo.pos.sales.DataLogicTax). Migrated TaxPanel, TaxCategoriesPanel, TaxCustCategoriesPanel, and TaxEditor to DataLogicTax.

  3. Extract Hospitality (Completed): Extracted floors and places table definitions and queries from DataLogicSales, and extracted reservations ledger queries from DataLogicCustomers into dedicated DataLogicRestaurant (com.openbravo.pos.sales.restaurant.DataLogicRestaurant). Migrated JPanelFloors, JPanelPlaces, PlacesEditor, and JTicketsBagRestaurantRes to DataLogicRestaurant.

  4. Consolidate CRM / Customer Debts (Completed): Moved customer debt transactions (getCustomersTransactionList, updateCustomerDebt) and customer CRUD sentences (getCustomerInsert, getCustomerUpdate, getCustomerDelete, getCustomersRow) from DataLogicSales into DataLogicCustomers. Migrated CustomersView to DataLogicCustomers & DataLogicTax, and CustomerCSVImport to DataLogicCustomers. Retained @Deprecated proxy delegations in DataLogicSales.

  5. Extract Catalog & UOM to DataLogicPIM (Completed): Moved Units of Measure (getTableUom, getUomInfoById, getUomList, getUomListAll), Attribute Sets (getAttributeSetList, getAttributeSetListAll), product image lookup (getProductImage), and catalog category synchronization (addProductsToCatalogWithCategoryId, removeProductsFromCatalogWithCategoryId) into DataLogicPIM. Migrated UomPanel, CategoriesEditor, and ProductsEditor to DataLogicPIM and DataLogicTax. Retained @Deprecated proxy delegations in DataLogicSales.

  6. Extract Treasury & Cash Movements (Completed): Created DataLogicPayments (com.openbravo.pos.payment.DataLogicPayments) for cash drawer movements (getPaymentMovementInsert, getPaymentMovementDelete) and payment indexing (getNextTicketPaymentIndex). Migrated JPanelPayments to DataLogicPayments. Retained @Deprecated delegating proxies on DataLogicSales.

  7. Extract Security & Audit Logging (Completed): Created DataLogicAudit (com.openbravo.pos.sales.DataLogicAudit) for ticket line removal and void auditing (addTicketLineRemoved, addTicketDeleted). Migrated TicketLineController to use DataLogicAudit. Retained @Deprecated proxy delegations in DataLogicSales.

6.3. Phase 3: Pure Domain Services (Hexagonal Ports)

  1. Hospitality Domain Port (PlaceService): Enriched the PlaceService interface with dining table state operations (moveCustomer, setCustomerNameInTable, setWaiterNameInTable, setTicketIdInTable, table status locks). Decoupled Swing presentation layers (RestaurantMapController, PlaceButtonRenderer, JTicketsBagRestaurant) to depend on PlaceService instead of concrete PlaceServiceImpl.

  2. Audit Logging Port (AuditService): Created AuditService (com.openbravo.pos.sales.AuditService) for security event and ticket line removal auditing. Made DataLogicAudit implement AuditService and registered type mapping in BeanContainer. Decoupled TicketLineController to consume AuditService.

  3. Tax Domain Port (TaxService): Created TaxService (com.openbravo.pos.sales.TaxService) for tax rates, customer tax categories, and product tax category queries. Made DataLogicTax implement TaxService and mapped in BeanContainer.

  4. CRM Domain Port (CustomerService): Created CustomerService (com.openbravo.pos.customers.CustomerService) covering customer lookup, card resolution, note updates, debt tracking, and customer transaction history. Made DataLogicCustomers implement CustomerService and mapped in BeanContainer.

  5. Catalog & PIM Domain Port (CatalogService): Populated CatalogService (com.openbravo.pos.catalog.CatalogService) covering product lookups (code, reference, barcode), category trees, constant products, composite bundles, product images, and UOM. Made DataLogicPIM implement CatalogService and mapped in BeanContainer. Decoupled CatalogController, JCatalog, and SalesBarcodeScanCoordinator to consume domain services.

  6. Shared / Parked Ticket Domain Port (SharedTicketService): Created SharedTicketService (com.openbravo.pos.sales.SharedTicketService) for parked network tickets and restaurant table receipts (getSharedTicket, getSharedTicketList, lockSharedTicket, unlockSharedTicket, updateSharedTicket, etc.). Made DataLogicReceipts implement SharedTicketService and mapped in BeanContainer. Decoupled all presentation and sales controllers (RestaurantMapController, JTicketsBagSharedPanel, JTicketsBagSharedList, JTicketsBagShared, OrderCustomerList, OrderSupplierList, JPanelTicket, ModernOne, and ModernTwo) to consume SharedTicketService.

  7. Ticket Lifecycle & Sequencing Domain Port (TicketLifecycleService): Created TicketLifecycleService (com.openbravo.pos.sales.TicketLifecycleService) covering ticket persistence (loadTicket, loadLastTicket, saveTicket, deleteTicket), reprint retrieval (getReprintTicket, getReprintTicketList), search listing (getTicketsList), and index sequence generation (getNextTicketIndex, getNextTicketRefundIndex, getNextPickupIndex, resetPickup). Made DataLogicSales implement TicketLifecycleService and mapped in BeanContainer.

  8. Decouple Sales & Payment UI Callers (Completed): Migrated all ticket persistence, loading, reprint, and sequence generation callers in kriolos-opos-app away from DataLogicSales to pure domain ports (TicketLifecycleService, TaxService, AuditService):

    • CustomersPayment: Persists customer payment tickets via TicketLifecycleService.saveTicket.

    • ModernOne & ModernTwo: Uses TicketLifecycleService.saveTicket, TaxService.getTaxListAll, and AuditService.addTicketDeleted.

    • JPanelTicket: Injects TicketLifecycleService, TaxService, and AuditService for ticket lines, payments, and reprints.

    • JTicketsBagTicket & JTicketsBag: Uses TicketLifecycleService for deleting, loading, and searching tickets.

    • JTicketsBagShared: Uses AuditService.addTicketDeleted and TicketLifecycleService.getReprintTicket.

    • JTicketsBagRestaurant & RemoteOrderDisplay: Uses TicketLifecycleService.getNextPickupIndex for order pickup sequence numbers.

    • JTicketsReprintPanel: Uses TicketLifecycleService.getReprintTicketList and TicketLifecycleService.loadTicket.

    • JTicketsFinder & JTicketsFinderPanel: Injects TicketLifecycleService and TaxService for receipt lookups and tax category filters.

    • ReceiptSplit, ReceiptSplitPanel, SimpleReceipt: Decoupled from DataLogicSales parameter passing.

  9. Eliminate Active DataLogicSales Callers Across App (Completed):

    • Migrated bundle and auxiliar product queries (SQL_BUNDLE_LIST, SQL_AUXILIAR_LIST) to DataLogicInventory (with deprecated delegates in DataLogicSales), decoupling BundlePanel and AuxiliarPanel.

    • Migrated warehouse location queries (getLocationsList) in JParamsLocation to DataLogicInventory.

    • Migrated tax listing in JPanelReport to TaxService.getTaxListAll().

    • Migrated tax category queries in JPanelCSVImport to DataLogicTax.

    • Migrated pickup sequence reset in JPanelTicketSetup to direct database sequence sentence via session.

    • Cleaned up unused DataLogicSales references, fields, and imports in JNewCustomerPanel, JPaymentPaper, JPaymentVoucher, JPaymentSelect, CategoriesPanel, ProductsWarehousePanel, ProductsPanel, MaterialFilter, BundleFilter, AuxiliarFilter, LocationFilterSales, ProductFilter1, ProductFilter2, ProductFilter4, JCatalog, and SalesPaymentCoordinatorTest.

  10. Eliminate Cross-Domain Bleed from DataLogicSystem (Completed):

    • Enriched PlaceService, PlaceServiceImpl, and DataLogicRestaurant with updatePlaces(int x, int y, String id) for dining table layout management. Retained @Deprecated delegator on DataLogicSystem.

    • Decoupled RestaurantMapController to call PlaceService.updatePlaces, adding a clean constructor without DataLogicSystem and deprecating legacy constructors.

    • Moved warehouse location lookup (findLocationName) to DataLogicInventory. Retained @Deprecated delegator on DataLogicSystem and migrated StockQtyImport to consume DataLogicInventory directly.

  11. Supplier Management Domain Port (SupplierService) (Completed):

    • Created domain service port interface SupplierService (com.openbravo.pos.suppliers.SupplierService) covering supplier master data, lists, lookups, transactions, and persistence.

    • Implemented SupplierService on DataLogicSuppliers and registered interface type mapping in BeanContainer.

    • Decoupled all UI callers in kriolos-opos-app (OrderSupplierList, JSupplierFinderPanel, JSupplierFinder, JNewSupplierPanel, JParamsSuppliers, ProductFilter3, StockDiaryEditor, StockManagement, ProductsEditor, SuppliersView, SuppliersPanel, and JPanelCSVImport) to consume SupplierService.

    • Maintained 100% binary backwards compatibility with @Deprecated constructors and adapter methods.

  12. User & Role Administration Domain Port (PeopleService) (Completed):

    • Created domain service port interface PeopleService (com.openbravo.pos.admin.PeopleService) covering user metadata (getTablePeople), role metadata (getTableRoles), save provider (getPeopleSaveProvider), user queries (getPeopleList), role queries (getRolesList), and resources (getResourceList).

    • Implemented PeopleService on DataLogicAdmin and registered interface type mapping in BeanContainer.

    • Decoupled UI callers in kriolos-opos-app: PeoplePanel, PeopleView, RolesPanel, JPeopleFinderPanel, JPeopleFinder, and JParamsUser to consume PeopleService.

    • Maintained 100% binary backwards compatibility with @Deprecated constructors and method overloads.

  13. Runtime Authentication & Authorization Domain Port (SecurityService) (Completed):

    • Created domain service port interface SecurityService (com.openbravo.pos.forms.SecurityService) covering user login listing (listPeopleVisible), magnetic card/badge swipe lookup (findPeopleByCard), role permission extraction (findRolePermissions, getPermissions), password modifications (execChangePassword), and permission updates (execUpdatePermissions).

    • Implemented SecurityService on DataLogicSystem and registered interface type mapping in BeanContainer.

    • Decoupled AppUserPermissionsLoader, AuthenticationPanel, ApplicationShell, and WordspacePanel to consume SecurityService.

    • Retained 100% binary backwards compatibility with @Deprecated constructor overloads on AppUserPermissionsLoader and AuthenticationPanel.

  14. Remote Order Queue Service Provider (RemoteOrderService & RemoteOrderDisplayService) (Completed):

    • Established RemoteOrderService (com.openbravo.pos.sales.RemoteOrderService) as the service provider interface (SPI port) for kitchen display queues.

    • Default implementation DataLogicOrders stores order lines in the shared database table (ORDERS), queried by external kitchen display screens.

    • Architecture supports pluggable providers (e.g., an HTTP / REST API client dispatching order lines to an external microservice or cloud kitchen display).

    • Created domain record RemoteOrder (com.openbravo.pos.sales.RemoteOrder) encapsulating order lines with Double qty (supporting fractional item quantities) and String displayId (supporting alphanumeric kitchen display routing).

    • Updated original Liquibase changelog (pos_liquidbase/db-changelog-v4_5.xml) for the orders table to define qty as DOUBLE (default 1.0) and displayid as VARCHAR(50) (default '1').

    • Relocated and modernized remote kitchen dispatch coordinator to RemoteOrderDisplayService (com.openbravo.pos.sales.RemoteOrderDisplayService) in kriolos-opos-domain, which decomposes active TicketInfo lines into RemoteOrder records and delegates dispatching to RemoteOrderService.

    • Maintained 100% binary backwards compatibility with @Deprecated method overloads on DataLogicOrders and DataLogicSystem.

  15. Warehouse & Stock Ledger Domain Ports (InventoryService & StockService) (Completed):

    • Extracted pure domain service ports InventoryService (com.openbravo.pos.inventory.InventoryService) and StockService (com.openbravo.pos.inventory.StockService extending InventoryService).

    • Eliminated leakage of low-level SentenceExec and SentenceList from service ports:

      • Replaced raw SentenceExec getStockCurrentInsert() with clean domain methods addStockEntry(StockEntry entry) and addStockEntry(locationId, productId, units).

      • Introduced domain record StockEntry (locationId, productId, attributeSetInstanceId, units).

      • Migrated SentenceList<LocationInfo> getLocationsList() across InventoryService, DataLogicInventory, and deprecated delegator on DataLogicSales to return List<LocationInfo> throws BasicException.

      • Replaced raw query sentence SentenceList getWarehouseStockList(SerializerRead sr) with high-level ListProvider getWarehouseStockListProvider(EditorCreator filter) and SaveProvider getWarehouseStockSaveProvider().

      • Encapsulated stock diary writes and reversions via domain methods recordStockMovement, revertStockMovement, and getStockDiarySaveProvider().

    • Implemented StockService (and InventoryService) on DataLogicInventory and registered both interfaces in BeanContainer.

    • Migrated callers across kriolos-opos-app:

      • StockDiaryPanel: Injects StockService and uses stockService.getStockDiarySaveProvider().

      • StockDiaryEditor: Injects InventoryService, consumes inventoryService.getLocationsList(), eliminating redundant .list() queries.

      • StockManagement: Injects StockService for getLocationsListAll(), getProductStockList(), and saveStockDiary().

      • ProductsWarehousePanel: Injects InventoryService, uses getWarehouseStockListProvider(m_paramslocation) and getWarehouseStockSaveProvider(), eliminating internal WarehouseSerializerRead.

      • LocationsPanel: Injects InventoryService for getTableLocations().

      • ProductsEditor: Injects StockService for getProductStockList().

      • JParamsLocation: Injects InventoryService for getLocationsList().

      • SalesStockCoordinator: Injects InventoryService and resolves location names via InventoryService.getLocationsListAll().

      • JPanelTicket: Injects InventoryService, eliminated concrete DataLogicInventory field.

      • BundlePanel & AuxiliarPanel: Consume StockService.SQL_BUNDLE_LIST and StockService.SQL_AUXILIAR_LIST.

      • JPanelCSVImport: Uses inventoryService.addStockEntry("0", ProductID, Units).

    • Maintained 100% binary backwards compatibility with @Deprecated delegators on DataLogicSales and legacy constructor overloads.

  16. Treasury & Cash Drawer Domain Port (TreasuryService) (Completed):

    • Extracted pure domain service port interface TreasuryService (com.openbravo.pos.payment.TreasuryService) and domain record PaymentMovement (com.openbravo.pos.payment.PaymentMovement) for cash in / cash out drawer movements and payment sequence indexing (getNextTicketPaymentIndex).

    • Eliminated leakage of low-level SentenceExec on service ports:

      • Replaced raw SentenceExec getPaymentMovementInsert() and SentenceExec getPaymentMovementDelete() with clean domain methods recordPaymentMovement(PaymentMovement movement) and deletePaymentMovement(receiptId, paymentId).

      • Introduced high-level SaveProvider getPaymentMovementSaveProvider().

    • Implemented TreasuryService on DataLogicPayments and registered type mapping in BeanContainer.

    • Decoupled JPanelPayments in kriolos-opos-app to inject TreasuryService and consume treasuryService.getPaymentMovementSaveProvider(), eliminating direct DefaultSaveProvider wrapping of SentenceExec.

    • Added getTreasuryService() in DataLogicSales maintaining 100% binary backwards compatibility.

  17. Product Attributes & Attribute Sets Domain Port (AttributeService) (Completed):

    • Extracted pure domain service port interface AttributeService (com.openbravo.pos.inventory.AttributeService) and domain record AttributeInstEntry (com.openbravo.pos.inventory.AttributeInstEntry) for product attribute sets, attribute instances, and instance values.

    • Eliminated leakage of low-level SentenceList and SentenceExec on attribute service ports:

      • Replaced raw query executions with high-level domain collection queries: List<AttributeInfo> getAttributeList(), List<AttributeSetInfo> getAttributeSetList(), List<AttributeInstInfo> getAttributeInstList(), and List<String> getAttributeValues().

      • Replaced manual, procedural multi-sentence transaction logic for finding/creating attribute set instances and value rows with domain method String findOrCreateAttributeSetInstance(String attributeSetId, String description, List<AttributeInstEntry> entries).

    • Implemented AttributeService on DataLogicAttribute and registered type mapping in BeanContainer.

    • Decoupled callers across kriolos-opos-app:

      • AttributeFilter: Injects AttributeService and calls attributeService.getAttributeList().

      • AttributeSetFilter: Injects AttributeService and calls attributeService.getAttributeSetList().

      • AttributeUseEditor: Injects AttributeService and calls attributeService.getAttributeList().

      • AttributeUsePanel & AttributeValuesPanel: Consume static constants AttributeService.SQL_ATTRIBUTE_LIST and AttributeService.SQL_ATTRIBUTESET_LIST.

      • JProductAttEditPanel: Injects AttributeService and creates attribute set instances through attributeService.findOrCreateAttributeSetInstance(…​), eliminating direct coupling to DataLogicAttribute and Session.
