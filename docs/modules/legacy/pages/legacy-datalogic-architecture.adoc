= Legacy Architecture — DataLogic Scope & Monolith Analysis
:description: Architectural analysis of all 13 legacy DataLogic classes in KriolOS POS, documenting broad scope flaws, cross-domain contamination, target domain model, and decoupling roadmap.

== Overview

In the original Openbravo POS / uniCenta architecture inherited by KriolOS POS, data access and transactional business logic are coupled directly to the database via 13 monolithic `DataLogic*` classes located in `kriolos-opos-domain`.

These classes are registered as singletons inside `BeanContainer` (extending `BeanFactoryDataSingle` or `BeanFactoryApp`). Over years of evolution, individual `DataLogic` classes accumulated multiple unrelated business concerns, resulting in **broad scope anti-patterns**, **cross-domain entity coupling**, and **hidden dependencies**.

This document serves as the baseline record of the legacy data layer architecture to guide the long-term objective of extracting clean, well-defined Business Modules.

== Scope Analysis & Anti-Patterns

=== `DataLogicSales`: The Super-Monolith (1,608 LOC)

`DataLogicSales` is the central God Object of the legacy system. Despite its name, it directly queries or mutates 29 database tables spanning **9 distinct business domains**:

1. **Ticket Lifecycle & Checkout (Core Sales Domain):**
   * Methods: `loadTicket`, `loadLastTicket`, `saveTicket`, `deleteTicket`, `getTicketsList`, `getReprintTicket`, `getReprintTicketList`.
   * Sequence Numbering: `getNextTicketIndex`, `getNextTicketRefundIndex`, `getNextPickupIndex`, `resetPickup`.
2. **Inventory & Stock Diary (Inventory Domain):**
   * Methods: `getProductStockState`, `getProductStockList`, `saveStockDiary`, `getStockDiaryInsert`, `getStockDiaryDelete`, `findProductStock`.
   * *Issue:* Stock diary recording and stock level checking are core inventory operations, yet they are stranded inside `DataLogicSales` rather than `DataLogicInventory`.
3. **Taxes & Fiscality (Finance/Tax Domain):**
   * Methods: `getTableTaxes`, `getTableTaxCategories`, `getTableTaxCustCategories`, `getTaxList`, `getTaxListAll`, `getTaxCategoriesList`.
4. **Customers & Debt (CRM Domain):**
   * Methods: `getCustomerInsert`, `getCustomerUpdate`, `getCustomerDelete`, `getCustomersTransactionList`, `updateCustomerDebt`.
   * *Issue:* Duplicates customer CRUD already provided by `DataLogicCustomers`.
5. **Locations & Warehouses (Master Data Domain):**
   * Methods: `getTableLocations`, `getLocationsList`, `getLocationsListAll`.
6. **Restaurant Layout (Hospitality Domain):**
   * Methods: `getFloorsList`, `getFloorTablesList` (queries `floors` and `places` tables).
7. **Product Master Data & UOM (PIM / Catalog Domain):**
   * Methods: `getTableUom`, `getUomInfoById`, `getUomList`, `getAttributeSetList`, `addProductsToCatalogWithCategoryId`, `removeProductsFromCatalogWithCategoryId`, `getProductImage`.
8. **Cash Movements & Drawers (Treasury Domain):**
   * Methods: `getPaymentMovementInsert`, `getPaymentMovementDelete`.
9. **Security & Audit Logging (Surveillance Domain):**
   * Methods: `addTicketLineRemoved`, `addTicketDeleted`.

[WARNING]
====
`DataLogicSales` contains tight couplings that bypass the bean container:
* Instantiates `DataLogicCustomers` directly inside transaction logic:
+
[source,java]
----
DataLogicCustomers customerDataLogic = new DataLogicCustomers();
customerDataLogic.init(sessionDB);
----
* Calls static methods across other DataLogics:
  ** `DataLogicVouchers.updateVoucherNonActive(...)`
  ** `DataLogicPIM.getProductsBundle(...)`
  ** `DataLogicPIM.getProductInfoExtById(...)`
====

=== `DataLogicSystem`: The Catch-All Trap (537 LOC)

Located in `com.openbravo.pos.forms`, `DataLogicSystem` combines platform-level infrastructure with high-level application logic:

* **System Metadata:** `getDBVersion()`, `findVersion()`.
* **Security & Auth (Overlapping with `DataLogicAdmin`):**
  * `listPeopleVisible()`, `findPeopleByCard()`, `getUser()`, `execChangePassword()`.
  * `getPermissions()`, `findRolePermissions()`, `execUpdatePermissions()`.
* **UI Resources & Assets:** `getTableResources()`, `getResourceAsBinary()`, `getResourceAsText()`, `setResource()`.
* **Kitchen Orders (Duplicate of `DataLogicOrders`):**
  * Implements `addOrder()`, `updateOrder()`, and `deleteOrder()`. `DataLogicOrders` only contains `addOrder()` and is largely orphaned.
* **Vouchers (Duplicate of `DataLogicVouchers`):**
  * Implements `getVouchersActiveList()` directly against the `vouchers` table rather than using `DataLogicVouchers`.

---

=== `DataLogicInventory` vs `DataLogicSales`: Anemic Fragmentation

* `DataLogicInventory` is only **72 lines** long and contains only 3 static statements for `stocklevel` and `stockcurrent`.
* All meaningful inventory operations—ledger transactions in `stockdiary`, stock valuations, multi-location stock calculations, and inventory adjustment statements—live inside `DataLogicSales`.
* Consequently, inventory UI screens (such as `StockManagement` and `StockDiaryEditor`) are forced to inject `DataLogicSales`, `DataLogicSystem`, `DataLogicSuppliers`, and `DataLogicPIM` concurrently.

---

=== `DataLogicReceipts`: Misnamed Component (420 LOC)

* The class name suggests receipt generation, printing, or fiscal receipt storage.
* In reality, it deals **100% with networked shared/parked tickets** (`sharedtickets` table):
  * `getSharedTicket()`, `getSharedTicketList()`, `insertSharedTicket()`, `updateSharedTicket()`, `lockSharedTicket()`, `unlockSharedTicket()`.
* Standard customer receipts and receipt reprints are handled entirely by `DataLogicSales` (`loadTicket`, `getReprintTicket`).

---

=== `DataLogicCustomers`: Hospitality Bleed

`DataLogicCustomers` manages customer accounts, cards, and tax numbers, but also includes table reservation management:
* `getReservationsList()`, `getReservationsInsert()`, `getReservationsUpdate()`, `getReservationsDelete()`.
* Interacts with `reservations` and `reservation_customers` tables (`CHAIRS`, `DATENEW`, etc.).
* Table reservations belong to the **Hospitality / Restaurant** domain, not the generic CRM domain.

== Target Business Module Architecture

To transform the legacy monolith into clean, modular components, the 13 `DataLogic` classes should be decoupled into bounded contexts:

[cols="2,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| Bounded Context | Responsibilities | Target Services
| **`system`** | Database version, system properties, binary & text resources | `SystemDomainService`
| **`auth`** | Users (`people`), roles, permissions, PIN/card authentication | `SecurityDomainService`
| **`catalog` (PIM)** | Products, categories, attributes, bundles, units of measure (UOM) | `CatalogDomainService`
| **`inventory`** | Warehouses, stock levels, stock diary ledger, stock adjustments | `InventoryDomainService`
| **`pricing-tax`** | Tax categories, tax rates, customer tax categories | `TaxDomainService`
| **`sales`** | Tickets, ticket lines, ticket sequence numbering, sales audit | `SalesDomainService`
| **`sales-parked`** | Networked shared/parked tickets, terminal locks, pickup tracking | `SharedTicketService`
| **`customer` (CRM)** | Customer profiles, cards, account debts, transaction ledger | `CustomerDomainService`
| **`procurement`** | Supplier master data, supplier transactions | `ProcurementDomainService`
| **`hospitality`** | Restaurant floors, tables/places, table reservations, kitchen orders | `HospitalityDomainService`
| **`treasury`** | Cash drawer movements, payment ledger, vouchers/gift cards | `PaymentDomainService`
| **`workforce` (EPM)** | Shifts, breaks, leaves, employee check-in/out | `WorkforceDomainService`
| **`data-exchange`** | CSV import/export routines | `ImportExportService`
|===

== Decoupling Roadmap

=== Phase 1: Deduplication & Bean Isolation
1. **Consolidate Kitchen Orders:** Remove duplicate order methods in `DataLogicSystem` and consolidate onto `DataLogicOrders` (or target `HospitalityDomainService`).
2. **Consolidate Vouchers:** Remove direct voucher queries in `DataLogicSystem` and `DataLogicSales`, routing them through `DataLogicVouchers`.
3. **Eliminate Ad-Hoc Instantiations:** Replace `new DataLogicCustomers()` inside `DataLogicSales` with type-safe `app.getBean(DataLogicCustomers.class)`.

=== Phase 2: Domain Extraction & Backwards-Compatible Facades
1. **Extract Inventory Logic (Completed):** Moved `saveStockDiary`, `getProductStockState`, `getProductStockList`, and `findProductStock` from `DataLogicSales` into `DataLogicInventory`. Migrated `LocationsPanel`, `StockDiaryPanel`, `StockDiaryEditor`, `StockManagement`, `ProductsEditor`, and `StockQtyImport` to `DataLogicInventory`.
2. **Extract Tax Logic (Completed):** Extracted tax queries (`getTaxList`, `getTaxCategoriesList`, `getTaxCustCategoriesList`) and table definitions (`getTableTaxes`, `getTableTaxCategories`, `getTableTaxCustCategories`) into dedicated `DataLogicTax` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.DataLogicTax`). Migrated `TaxPanel`, `TaxCategoriesPanel`, `TaxCustCategoriesPanel`, and `TaxEditor` to `DataLogicTax`.
3. **Extract Hospitality (Completed):** Extracted `floors` and `places` table definitions and queries from `DataLogicSales`, and extracted `reservations` ledger queries from `DataLogicCustomers` into dedicated `DataLogicRestaurant` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.restaurant.DataLogicRestaurant`). Migrated `JPanelFloors`, `JPanelPlaces`, `PlacesEditor`, and `JTicketsBagRestaurantRes` to `DataLogicRestaurant`.
5. **Consolidate CRM / Customer Debts (Completed):** Moved customer debt transactions (`getCustomersTransactionList`, `updateCustomerDebt`) and customer CRUD sentences (`getCustomerInsert`, `getCustomerUpdate`, `getCustomerDelete`, `getCustomersRow`) from `DataLogicSales` into `DataLogicCustomers`. Migrated `CustomersView` to `DataLogicCustomers` & `DataLogicTax`, and `CustomerCSVImport` to `DataLogicCustomers`. Retained `@Deprecated` proxy delegations in `DataLogicSales`.
6. **Extract Catalog & UOM to DataLogicPIM (Completed):** Moved Units of Measure (`getTableUom`, `getUomInfoById`, `getUomList`, `getUomListAll`), Attribute Sets (`getAttributeSetList`, `getAttributeSetListAll`), product image lookup (`getProductImage`), and catalog category synchronization (`addProductsToCatalogWithCategoryId`, `removeProductsFromCatalogWithCategoryId`) into `DataLogicPIM`. Migrated `UomPanel`, `CategoriesEditor`, and `ProductsEditor` to `DataLogicPIM` and `DataLogicTax`. Retained `@Deprecated` proxy delegations in `DataLogicSales`.
7. **Extract Treasury & Cash Movements (Completed):** Created `DataLogicPayments` (`com.openbravo.pos.payment.DataLogicPayments`) for cash drawer movements (`getPaymentMovementInsert`, `getPaymentMovementDelete`) and payment indexing (`getNextTicketPaymentIndex`). Migrated `JPanelPayments` to `DataLogicPayments`. Retained `@Deprecated` delegating proxies on `DataLogicSales`.
8. **Extract Security & Audit Logging (Completed):** Created `DataLogicAudit` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.DataLogicAudit`) for ticket line removal and void auditing (`addTicketLineRemoved`, `addTicketDeleted`). Migrated `TicketLineController` to use `DataLogicAudit`. Retained `@Deprecated` proxy delegations in `DataLogicSales`.

=== Phase 3: Pure Domain Services (Hexagonal Ports)
1. **Hospitality Domain Port (`PlaceService`):** Enriched the `PlaceService` interface with dining table state operations (`moveCustomer`, `setCustomerNameInTable`, `setWaiterNameInTable`, `setTicketIdInTable`, table status locks). Decoupled Swing presentation layers (`RestaurantMapController`, `PlaceButtonRenderer`, `JTicketsBagRestaurant`) to depend on `PlaceService` instead of concrete `PlaceServiceImpl`.
2. **Audit Logging Port (`AuditService`):** Created `AuditService` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.AuditService`) for security event and ticket line removal auditing. Made `DataLogicAudit` implement `AuditService` and registered type mapping in `BeanContainer`. Decoupled `TicketLineController` to consume `AuditService`.
3. **Tax Domain Port (`TaxService`):** Created `TaxService` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.TaxService`) for tax rates, customer tax categories, and product tax category queries. Made `DataLogicTax` implement `TaxService` and mapped in `BeanContainer`.
4. **CRM Domain Port (`CustomerService`):** Created `CustomerService` (`com.openbravo.pos.customers.CustomerService`) covering customer lookup, card resolution, note updates, debt tracking, and customer transaction history. Made `DataLogicCustomers` implement `CustomerService` and mapped in `BeanContainer`.
5. **Catalog & PIM Domain Port (`CatalogService`):** Populated `CatalogService` (`com.openbravo.pos.catalog.CatalogService`) covering product lookups (code, reference, barcode), category trees, constant products, composite bundles, product images, and UOM. Made `DataLogicPIM` implement `CatalogService` and mapped in `BeanContainer`. Decoupled `CatalogController`, `JCatalog`, and `SalesBarcodeScanCoordinator` to consume domain services.
6. **Shared / Parked Ticket Domain Port (`SharedTicketService`):** Created `SharedTicketService` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.SharedTicketService`) for parked network tickets and restaurant table receipts (`getSharedTicket`, `getSharedTicketList`, `lockSharedTicket`, `unlockSharedTicket`, `updateSharedTicket`, etc.). Made `DataLogicReceipts` implement `SharedTicketService` and mapped in `BeanContainer`. Decoupled all presentation and sales controllers (`RestaurantMapController`, `JTicketsBagSharedPanel`, `JTicketsBagSharedList`, `JTicketsBagShared`, `OrderCustomerList`, `OrderSupplierList`, `JPanelTicket`, `ModernOne`, and `ModernTwo`) to consume `SharedTicketService`.
7. **Ticket Lifecycle & Sequencing Domain Port (`TicketLifecycleService`):** Created `TicketLifecycleService` (`com.openbravo.pos.sales.TicketLifecycleService`) covering ticket persistence (`loadTicket`, `loadLastTicket`, `saveTicket`, `deleteTicket`), reprint retrieval (`getReprintTicket`, `getReprintTicketList`), search listing (`getTicketsList`), and index sequence generation (`getNextTicketIndex`, `getNextTicketRefundIndex`, `getNextPickupIndex`, `resetPickup`). Made `DataLogicSales` implement `TicketLifecycleService` and mapped in `BeanContainer`.
8. **Decouple Sales & Payment UI Callers (Completed):** Migrated all ticket persistence, loading, reprint, and sequence generation callers in `kriolos-opos-app` away from `DataLogicSales` to pure domain ports (`TicketLifecycleService`, `TaxService`, `AuditService`):
   * `CustomersPayment`: Persists customer payment tickets via `TicketLifecycleService.saveTicket`.
   * `ModernOne` & `ModernTwo`: Uses `TicketLifecycleService.saveTicket`, `TaxService.getTaxListAll`, and `AuditService.addTicketDeleted`.
   * `JPanelTicket`: Injects `TicketLifecycleService`, `TaxService`, and `AuditService` for ticket lines, payments, and reprints.
   * `JTicketsBagTicket` & `JTicketsBag`: Uses `TicketLifecycleService` for deleting, loading, and searching tickets.
   * `JTicketsBagShared`: Uses `AuditService.addTicketDeleted` and `TicketLifecycleService.getReprintTicket`.
   * `JTicketsBagRestaurant` & `RemoteOrderDisplay`: Uses `TicketLifecycleService.getNextPickupIndex` for order pickup sequence numbers.
   * `JTicketsReprintPanel`: Uses `TicketLifecycleService.getReprintTicketList` and `TicketLifecycleService.loadTicket`.
   * `JTicketsFinder` & `JTicketsFinderPanel`: Injects `TicketLifecycleService` and `TaxService` for receipt lookups and tax category filters.
   * `ReceiptSplit`, `ReceiptSplitPanel`, `SimpleReceipt`: Decoupled from `DataLogicSales` parameter passing.

   * Migrated warehouse location queries (`getLocationsList`) in `JParamsLocation` to `DataLogicInventory`.
   * Migrated tax listing in `JPanelReport` to `TaxService.getTaxListAll()`.
   * Migrated tax category queries in `JPanelCSVImport` to `DataLogicTax`.
   * Migrated pickup sequence reset in `JPanelTicketSetup` to direct database sequence sentence via session.
   * Cleaned up unused `DataLogicSales` references, fields, and imports in `JNewCustomerPanel`, `JPaymentPaper`, `JPaymentVoucher`, `JPaymentSelect`, `CategoriesPanel`, `ProductsWarehousePanel`, `ProductsPanel`, `MaterialFilter`, `BundleFilter`, `AuxiliarFilter`, `LocationFilterSales`, `ProductFilter1`, `ProductFilter2`, `ProductFilter4`, `JCatalog`, and `SalesPaymentCoordinatorTest`.

   * Enriched `PlaceService`, `PlaceServiceImpl`, and `DataLogicRestaurant` with `updatePlaces(int x, int y, String id)` for dining table layout management. Retained `@Deprecated` delegator on `DataLogicSystem`.
   * Decoupled `RestaurantMapController` to call `PlaceService.updatePlaces`, adding a clean constructor without `DataLogicSystem` and deprecating legacy constructors.
   * Moved warehouse location lookup (`findLocationName`) to `DataLogicInventory`. Retained `@Deprecated` delegator on `DataLogicSystem` and migrated `StockQtyImport` to consume `DataLogicInventory` directly.
